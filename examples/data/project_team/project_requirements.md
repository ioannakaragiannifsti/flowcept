# Customer Records Cleanup Project

Select exactly three people for a four-week project running from 1 October through
31 October 2026.

## Mandatory constraints

- Each person must be active and available for the entire project.
- Each person must have at least 24 hours per week remaining after overlapping assignments.
- Each selected person must contribute at least one required skill at proficiency 4 or higher.
- The team must cover Python, SQL, communication, and project coordination at proficiency 4 or higher.
- At least one member must have completed a data-migration project.
- Planned effort is 24 hours per person per week for four weeks.
- Total labor cost must not exceed USD 18,500.

## Preference order

1. Satisfy every mandatory constraint.
2. Prefer recent skill use and successful data-migration experience.
3. Prefer stronger manager feedback.
4. If teams remain tied, select the lower-cost team.

The decision must show its cost calculation, skill coverage, supporting document citations,
and reasons for rejecting close alternatives.
